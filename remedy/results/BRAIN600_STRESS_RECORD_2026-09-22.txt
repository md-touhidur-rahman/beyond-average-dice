BEYOND AVERAGE DICE — BRAIN600 CROSS-REGIME STRESS TEST
Date: 2026-09-22

ROLE
Brain600 is a separate cross-regime stress test / boundary-transfer analysis.
It is not used for method selection, threshold tuning, or Brain400
confirmatory validation.

REGIME
600 Brain images.
Six controlled language conditions:
H0, H1, H2, L3, L4, L5.

This differs from the Brain400 P20 / SAM-B multimask setting.
Brain600 uses the controlled six-condition regime and SAM-H single-mask
setting.

Patient correspondence is unavailable and is not assumed.

FROZEN METHOD

1 - mean pairwise IoU among description-specific localization boxes.

With six descriptions, the score uses all 15 pairwise localization-box IoUs.

0.264446087201

INTERPRETATION
The frozen cross-description localization-disagreement score transferred
across a different language regime and SAM setting as a useful risk-ranking
signal.

At the Brain400-frozen threshold, Brain600 high-risk cases were substantially
enriched for catastrophic variation, while retained cases had substantially
lower catastrophic prevalence.

The operating characteristics did not transfer identically: sensitivity was
0.710526 rather than the approximately 0.80 development target.

This result does not establish:
- universal failure detection;
- that disagreement proves an individual prediction is wrong;
- that agreement proves correctness;
- automatic correction of failed segmentations;
- clinical utility;
- architecture-level causality.

Brain600 remains a stress test / boundary condition rather than independent
patient-level confirmatory validation.

NO BRAIN600 TUNING
Brain600 was not used to:
- select the risk score;
- select the threshold;
- modify the catastrophic definition;
- choose the primary Brain400 endpoint;
- tune a replacement localization strategy.
