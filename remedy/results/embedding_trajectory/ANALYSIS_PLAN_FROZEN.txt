EMBEDDING-TRAJECTORY MECHANISM ANALYSIS PLAN
============================================

STATUS
------
PRE-SPECIFIED BEFORE INSPECTION OF THE SCIENTIFIC OUTPUT OF JOB 1820091.

PURPOSE
-------
Test whether controlled interpolation between two projected text
representations induces an ordered change in M2IB localization.

This analysis is mechanistic and development-only.

No ground truth, Dice outcomes, held-out outcomes, or outcome-based
method/threshold tuning may be used.

EXPERIMENTAL STRUCTURE
----------------------
Cases: 12 development cases
Prompt pairs: 20 per case
Seeds: 5 per case-prompt pair
Interpolation doses: 0.00, 0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 1.00

Planned trajectories:
12 * 20 * 5 = 1200

The five observations within a trajectory are NOT independent
replicates.

The 1200 trajectories are NOT to be treated as independent units
for formal inference.

PRIMARY ENDPOINT
----------------
Raw-heatmap correlation distance from endpoint A.

For each trajectory define:

    t = [0.00, 0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 1.00]

and

    D_A(t) = correlation_distance(H(t), H(0))

The PRIMARY trajectory statistic is:

    Spearman correlation between t and D_A(t)

This metric is fixed before result inspection.

PRIMARY DESCRIPTIVE SUMMARIES
-----------------------------
Across all 1200 planned trajectories report:

1. number successfully evaluated
2. mean trajectory-level Spearman rho
3. median trajectory-level Spearman rho
4. IQR and range of trajectory-level rho
5. number and proportion with rho > 0
6. number and proportion with rho = 0
7. number and proportion with rho < 0
8. number and proportion whose D_A sequence is non-decreasing
   across all five interpolation doses

No trajectory may be excluded because it is non-monotonic,
unexpected, noisy, or weak.

Only genuine technical failures such as missing/corrupt output or
non-finite values may be excluded, and every such failure must be
counted and reported.

SECONDARY ENDPOINT 1
--------------------
Raw-heatmap MAE from endpoint A.

Analyze using the same trajectory-level Spearman and monotonicity
summaries as the primary endpoint.

SECONDARY ENDPOINT 2
--------------------
Bounding-box instability from endpoint A, defined using 1-IoU.

Analyze using the same trajectory-level summaries.

This endpoint is downstream of heatmap generation and the
heatmap-to-box discretization procedure and is therefore secondary.

SYMMETRIC ENDPOINT-B CHECK
--------------------------
For the primary raw-heatmap correlation-distance metric also define:

    D_B(t) = correlation_distance(H(t), H(1))

Pre-specified expectation:

    D_B(t) decreases as t increases.

Report trajectory-level Spearman correlations between t and D_B(t).

A negative association is the predicted direction.

ENDPOINT-PROGRESS ANALYSIS
--------------------------
Pre-specified secondary mechanistic analysis:

    P(t) = D_A(t) / [D_A(t) + D_B(t)]

where the denominator is non-zero.

Expected endpoint behavior:

    P(0) = 0
    P(1) = 1

Evaluate whether P(t) increases with interpolation dose using
trajectory-level Spearman correlation and report the complete
distribution.

This is explicitly SECONDARY, not the primary endpoint.

HIERARCHICAL SUMMARIZATION
--------------------------
Raw trajectories must not be treated as independent inferential
replicates.

Summarize results hierarchically:

Level 1:
    1200 case x prompt-pair x seed trajectories

Level 2:
    240 case x prompt-pair summaries,
    collapsing the five matched seed realizations

Level 3:
    12 case-level summaries

The 12 development cases are the PRIMARY replication level for
case-level interpretation/inference.

The 240 case-prompt-pair observations may be reported as an
intermediate descriptive level but will not replace the 12 cases
as the primary replication level merely to increase apparent n.

INTERPRETATION RULE
-------------------
No arbitrary numerical effect-size threshold is introduced after
seeing the data.

Interpretation will use the pre-specified directional structure.

STRONG SUPPORT:
Predominantly positive D_A trajectory associations, clearly positive
central tendency, recurrence across cases, and corresponding negative
D_B associations / positive endpoint-progress associations.

MIXED SUPPORT:
Positive overall directional behavior but substantial reproducible
heterogeneity or non-monotonicity across cases/trajectories.

NO SUPPORT FOR ORDERED MEDIATION:
No consistent directional relationship between embedding interpolation
and localization displacement.

EVIDENCE AGAINST SIMPLE MONOTONIC MEDIATION:
Substantial reproducible non-monotonic behavior incompatible with a
simple ordered trajectory.

NON-MONOTONIC TRAJECTORIES
--------------------------
Non-monotonic trajectories are scientific observations.

They must:
- remain in the analysis,
- be counted,
- have their proportion reported,
- not be relabeled as noise merely because they violate expectation.

FIGURE-SELECTION RULE
---------------------
No trajectory may be selected for a manuscript figure because it
looks especially monotonic or visually compelling after inspection.

Any qualitative trajectory illustration must use either:
1. a rule fixed independently of trajectory appearance, or
2. a presentation explicitly showing the distribution/range of
   trajectory behaviors.

INFERENCE RESTRICTION
---------------------
No p-value or confidence statement may treat the 1200 trajectories
as 1200 independent experimental units.

Any subsequent formal inferential procedure must preserve the
clustered/case structure and will be clearly distinguished from the
pre-specified descriptive trajectory analysis.
